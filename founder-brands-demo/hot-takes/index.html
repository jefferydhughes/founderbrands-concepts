The three ways outbound gets ignored.

Everyone's doing at least one of these. Most founders are doing all three.

Pitch-slapping DMs

Connect, pitch, follow up, follow up again. Instant block. Buyers can spot a sequence in one line.

Posting from the company page

Your logo has no friends. People follow people. A company page talks to its own employees.

Bought lead lists

Cold names who've never heard of you, reached by a tool that sounds like a tool.

The One-Hour Engine

Content and conversation, in that order. Your posts give buyers a reason to know you. Your outbound specialist starts conversations with comments that actually say something, an observation, a number, a disagreement, then moves to the DM once they recognise your name.

  2. 02 PublishDaily posts on LinkedIn and X, written from that hour in your voice. You approve. Unlimited revisions.
